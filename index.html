How to play

Choose a cracker, then tap the screen. Rockets whistle up to where you tap and burst into peonies, rings and golden willows. Anar fountains and chakra spinners sit on the ground where you place them. Hold your finger down with the sparkler selected to draw with light. Bombs have a short fuse, so watch the spark.

Why a virtual Diwali?

Real crackers add smoke and noise that is hard on people with asthma, babies, elders and pets. This simulator lets you enjoy the colour and sound of Diwali anywhere, any time of year.

Questions

Is it free? Yes. It runs in your browser on phone, tablet and desktop, with nothing to install.
Does it work offline? Once the page has loaded, the game runs without a connection.
