How the physics works

The car weighs 1,900 kg and its motor makes 750 kW. At low speed the motor is limited by torque (30 kN at the wheels). Above about 90 km/h it is limited by power, so thrust falls as speed rises. Tyre grip is friction times load, with some downforce added at speed. Ask for more thrust than the tyres can hold and they spin, which loses force. Air drag grows with the square of speed. Turn traction control off to feather the throttle yourself.

Tips for a fast run

Roll onto the throttle quickly, then hold it just under the green line until the bar can rise. Past about 100 km/h you can hold full throttle. The thruster adds 15 kN for 1.5 seconds, once per run. Press Full screen for the best view.
