Levels and maps

Eight levels take you through four maps: Desert Dusk, Neon City at night, Arctic Dawn and the Salt Flats. Levels change the distance (1/8 mile to half a mile), grip (dry, wet, ice), and headwind. Earn up to three stars on each level to unlock the next one. From level 4 on, traction control is switched off.

How the physics works

The car weighs 1,900 kg and its motor makes 750 kW. At low speed the motor is limited by torque (30 kN at the wheels). Above about 90 km/h it is limited by power, so thrust falls as speed rises. Tyre grip is friction times load, with some downforce at speed, and friction depends on the road: dry asphalt, wet asphalt or snow. Ask for more thrust than the tyres can hold and they spin, which loses force. Air drag grows with the square of speed and headwind adds to it.

Tips for a fast run

Roll onto the throttle quickly, then hold it just under the green line until the bar can rise. On low-grip levels the green line sits low, so be patient. The thruster adds 15 kN for 1.5 seconds, once per run. Press Full screen for the best view.
